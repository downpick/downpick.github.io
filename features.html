What Downpick does

Four things, in order: connect, look, ask, take away.

01 · Connect

Every server, open at once

Add a connection once and it stays in the sidebar — staging next to production next to the local copy, each with a dot telling you whether it's live. Passwords go into the vault, not into a config file.

⬡ Ridgeline Analytics (staging) · idle
⬡ Ridgeline Coffee (local) · connected
⬡ Telemetry · idle

02 · Look

See the shape of your data

The explorer goes all the way down — databases, schemas, tables, columns and their types. On MongoDB, collections and their fields. Double-click any table to query it.

03 · Ask

Write it, or describe it

The editor highlights and autocompletes SQL and MongoDB shell syntax, formats on a keystroke, and runs the selection if you've selected something.

Or press ✦ Ask AI and say what you're after. It reads your table and column names, writes the query, and waits. Running it is still your call — and a DELETE with no WHERE clause still asks twice.

04 · Take away

Results that behave

A grid that scrolls smoothly at a hundred thousand rows, with types under every column heading. Mongo documents flatten into a table, or open as a tree, one document at a time. Then copy it as a table into Slack or Sheets, or export CSV or Excel.

And the rest

An encrypted vault

Every connection password is encrypted behind one master password, locked on launch and re-locked after a period of inactivity you choose.

Cancel and timeouts

Stop a long query with a keystroke, or set a timeout — 30 seconds by default — that stops it for you.

Confirmation on destructive statements

UPDATE or DELETE with no WHERE clause, and Mongo updateMany or deleteMany with an empty filter, ask before they run.

Tabs, restored

Your open query tabs, pane sizes, and window position come back exactly as you left them.

Errors, verbatim

The server's own message, in monospace, with the line number — not a friendlier rewrite of it.

Keyboard first

Run, cancel, format, new connection, lock the vault, open settings — all on shortcuts, all on the menu bar too.

Your choice of AI provider

OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Azure, or a local model on your own machine. Keys live in the vault.

Nothing phones home

No telemetry, no account, no network access from the window that renders your data.
